# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults of any age, gender, or background who are interested in evidence-based skincare. Primary context: browsing at home on mobile or desktop, looking for products they can trust. They read ingredient lists, distrust inflated claims, and appreciate transparency over marketing language. Secondary context: people in Mexico with skin concerns specific to the region (UV intensity, humidity, diverse phototypes III–V, melasma, acne).

## Product Purpose

Protect Diversity is a Mexican dermocosmetic brand selling minimalist, dermatologically approved skincare formulated for the real diversity of Mexican skin. Products are vegan, biodegradable, and made in CDMX. The website is the primary brand presence and sales channel (pre-Shopify migration), conveying scientific credibility, ethical values, and accessibility — not luxury, not clinic-cold.

## Brand Personality

Científica · Honesta · Directa · Mexicana · Ética · Vegana · Accesible

Voice: first-person plural ("formulamos", "nacimos"), concrete not aspirational. Specific ingredient names and percentages over category buzzwords. No promises that can't be substantiated. Warmth comes from proximity and honesty, not softness.

## Anti-references

- **Luxury fantasy (La Mer, Tatcha)**: aspirational staging, inaccessible pricing theater, glow-and-gloss aesthetics. Protect Diversity is rigorous, not indulgent.
- **Clinical-cold pharmacy (Cerave, Bioderma generic)**: functional but personality-less. The brand has a point of view.
- **TikTok-viral beauty (The INKEY List hype cycle)**: trend-chasing, noise-driven, ephemeral. Protect Diversity is grounded.
- **Generic warm-neutral AI skincare**: cream/sand/beige body bg with rounded cards and eyebrow-on-every-section scaffold. The existing brand already breaks from this with its cacao/terracota palette — preserve that distinction.

## Design Principles

1. **Ingredients over promises.** The copy shows the work (names, percentages, functions). Design should reinforce this: information density is a feature, not a flaw.
2. **Proximity, not aspiration.** The brand speaks to the user as a peer who knows more about skin, not a luxury object on a pedestal.
3. **Mexican identity is specific, not decorative.** CDMX origin, phototypes III–V, regional UV conditions — these are real design inputs, not brand flavor.
4. **Ethical stance is structural.** Vegan, biodegradable, cruelty-free are defaults, not badges. Design treats them as ambient facts, not trust-building marketing.
5. **Restraint earns trust.** Every element on screen should earn its presence. Empty space is not absence; it is the brand's confidence that the product speaks for itself.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum.
- Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against background at all breakpoints.
- All interactive elements keyboard-accessible.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected on all animations.
- Language: Spanish (es-MX). Inclusive language by product benefit, not demographic identity.
